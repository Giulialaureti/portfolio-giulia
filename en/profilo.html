Profile

Designing connections
between people, content
and context.

I work as an Experience Designer, with experience in UX research, digital design, accessibility and cultural events. I translate complex needs into understandable, inclusive experiences, connecting method, content and delivery.

My work moves between digital and physical environments. Since 2021 I've been designing and developing websites and platforms as a freelancer, following every project from defining the experience through to maintenance. Research, information architecture, content and interfaces are, to me, parts of the same journey, built around the people who will actually use what I design.

Alongside my digital practice I pursue two complementary directions: as co-founder and vice-president of TracciaRoma, I design cultural experiences and events dedicated to participation and to celebrating local heritage; at university, I teach and collaborate on courses related to data visualisation, artificial intelligence, computer science and digital marketing. I've also worked in Human-Machine Interaction research for an immersive, multisensory installation.

These different contexts have taught me to move from research to delivery without losing the thread: listening to different perspectives, making complex content legible, coordinating technical constraints, and checking decisions over time.

My approach

From understanding to delivery.

Understand the context

I gather needs, content and points of view, observing closely the context the project will take shape in. I try to understand not just what needs to be built, but who will use it, update it, or move through it over time.

Give the system shape

I organise information and relationships, then translate them into paths, interfaces, content and physical elements that work together. Every choice needs to help people find their way, without adding unnecessary complexity to the system.

Verify and sustain

I prototype, test and develop, gathering real feedback whenever the project allows and observing actual use to iterate on decisions. I treat maintenance and independence as part of the outcome from the design stage onward.
