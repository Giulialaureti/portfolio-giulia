Cookie Policy

A cookie is a small text file that visited sites send to the browser, where it is stored and then sent back to the same sites on the next visit. This site uses:

  • Necessary technical storage (does not require consent): stores your light/dark theme preference and the choice made on the cookie banner locally on your device. It is not shared with anyone.
